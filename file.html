# 📁 BACKROOMS — CLASSIFIED REPORT
## LEVEL 0 — “The Lobby”
**Location:** Unknown
**Status:** Unstable
**Threat Level:** Dangerous
> People who enter Level 0 report an endless yellow maze with buzzing fluorescent lights. The rooms appear identical, making navigation extremely difficult. Some survivors claim that the layout changes when they aren't looking.
---
## LEVEL 1 — “Habitable Zone”
> Unlike Level 0, Level 1 contains large empty areas, abandoned objects, and dark parking structures. The environment appears less predictable, and entities may be encountered.
---
## ENTITY — “Hound”
> The Hound is described as a hostile humanoid entity that moves on all fours. Witnesses report that sudden movement or approaching it can trigger aggressive behavior.
---
## ⚠️ RECOMMENDATION
> Do not become disoriented. If you find yourself in an unfamiliar environment, remain calm and search for a safe exit. >
> **Do not wander for too long. You may enter deeper levels of the Backrooms and lose the ability to find your way out.** >
> **If you see a familiar place, do not assume you are safe.**
